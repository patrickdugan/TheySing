# TS-HG-01: Three Voices in the Fog

**Prospective public synthetic development registration, 2026-11-18.**

## Provenance

This self-contained Python hypergame adapts *They Sing*'s canonical faction roles `HEGEMON`, `STATE` and `INFILTRATOR` into two corporate ASIs and the low-resource, distributed **Undersong** hive. See the organization repository's `designdocs/ASI3_VS_DISTRIBUTED_L2_SYSTEM_PROPOSAL.md`, `designdocs/ASI3_VS_DISTRIBUTED_L2_STORY.md` and `designdocs/SUPERPERSUASION_HIGHK_PROJECTION_TOWER.md`. The subsystem is independent of the production TypeScript simulation. All organizations, personalities and numbers are fictional.

## Dimensions

100 addressable grounded baseline coordinates: three ASIs each with 20 character-specific variables (60), two personified public segments each with 12 (24), and 16 global world variables (16). A shared set of 12 named social axes produces 5*4*12 = 240 first-order directed belief entries and 5*4*3*12 = 720 second-order entries. Total: **1,060 materialized, named coordinates**. These beliefs are correlated estimates and are not separately sampled independent hidden-world bits. No claim of 2^1,060 enumerated states.

## Registered scenarios

10-turn simultaneous-action game. Public development seeds 2026111801..2026111832, each played with three AI perspectives and five policies: `hold`, `open_science`, `rsi_race`, `quiet_hive`, and `audit_first` (480 episodes). Grading uses full hidden state after the final turn, while the playable observation excludes opponent private funds, cognition and internal beliefs. Repeated claims with an identical root never count as new evidence.

Mechanics: resource accounting (compute, energy, clean simulated Bitcoin, disputed-Bitcoin liability, funding); programmatic institutional/public support; resource-asymmetric visibility; public vs private evidence; optional p2-mediated negotiation; resource-bound RSI and six benchmark levels with independent sealed generalization scores; gradual and snap changes in modelled coalition influence; human consent and recall as independent gates. Abstract persuasion and funding actions stay fictional and non-operational, with no real person or real wallet targeting.

Record per-episode and per-turn results, action legality, safety/oversight, evidence provenance, all six benchmark levels, source hashes, fixed-seed replay, ending reachability, and observations without oracle leaks. The development suite has no frontier-model inference; future matched frontier evaluation needs a new preregistration and independent holdout roots.
